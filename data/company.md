# Kestrel Cloud B.V. — Company Profile

**Kestrel Cloud B.V.** is an Amsterdam-based B2B SaaS company that builds and operates the **Kestrel Platform**, a customer data integration and analytics platform. The platform ingests data from SaaS tools and databases, models it into a governed data layer, and exposes it through dashboards, scheduled reports, and REST/GraphQL APIs. Founded in 2019, Kestrel now employs approximately 180 people, with headquarters at Gustav Mahlerlaan, Amsterdam, and engineering teams in Amsterdam and Lisbon. Roughly 320 customers, mostly mid-market and enterprise organisations in the EU and UK, run on the platform.

**Leadership:** Mara Visser (CEO), Tom Aldridge (CISO), Elena Rossi (Data Protection Officer, dpo@kestrelcloud.example). The company is profitable since 2025, raised a €28M Series B in 2024, and carries no debt.

**Hosting and residency:** The platform runs on AWS. The primary region is eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) with disaster recovery in eu-west-1 (Dublin). A US region (us-east-1) has been available on request for US customers since Q1 2026. Infrastructure is shared; tenant data is isolated through per-tenant encryption keys in AWS KMS and row-level tenancy controls.

**Assurance:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified (certificate KC-27001-2024-118, issued by Brightline Certification B.V., valid to 11 March 2027). SOC 2 Type II report covering 1 January – 31 December 2025 (Security, Availability, Confidentiality), audited by Vandermeer & Holt LLP. Cyber Essentials Plus (UK), renewed January 2026. GDPR-aligned with a standard DPA including SCCs. Annual third-party penetration test (most recently Sekura Labs, March 2026). Not held or not applicable: FedRAMP, HIPAA BAA, PCI DSS; ISO 27017/27018 are planned for 2027.

**Operating commitments:** 99.9% monthly uptime SLA with service credits; 24/7 Sev-1 support; RPO 1 hour, RTO 4 hours; data residency in the EU by default; customer data never used to train AI models; subprocessor changes notified 30 days in advance.

**Commercial defaults:** liability capped at 12 months' fees, IP-infringement indemnity offered, 12–36 month terms, net-30 payment. Deviations require Legal approval.
