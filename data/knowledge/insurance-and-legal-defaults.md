# Insurance Coverage and Standard Legal and Commercial Terms

**Owner:** Legal (General Counsel) | **Purpose:** reference for sales and security questionnaire responses. Items marked **[Legal approval required]** must not be varied in a customer response without written sign-off from Legal.

## 1. Insurance

Kestrel Cloud B.V. maintains the following policies, all with Allianz, renewed annually on 1 April:

| Policy | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cyber liability (including data breach response, business interruption, third-party liability, regulatory defence) | €5,000,000 per claim and in aggregate | Covers notification costs, forensics, and credit monitoring |
| Professional indemnity / errors and omissions | €2,000,000 per claim | Covers technology services |
| Commercial general liability | €2,500,000 per occurrence | Includes public and product liability |

Certificates of insurance are available on request from legal@kestrelcloud.example. Requests to increase limits or to name customers as additional insured are **[Legal approval required]**.

## 2. Standard contractual terms

Kestrel's Master Subscription Agreement (MSA v7, March 2026) and Data Processing Agreement (v4.2) are the default paper for all customers. Key commercial and legal defaults:

| Term | Standard position | Variation |
|---|---|---|
| Contract term | 12, 24, or 36 months; auto-renewal for successive 12-month terms unless 60 days' notice | Legal approval for terms outside range |
| Pricing model | Annual platform fee plus usage tiers based on rows processed and connectors | Sales discretion within approved discount matrix |
| Payment | Annual in advance; net 30 days from invoice; EUR or GBP; 1.5% monthly late interest | Quarterly billing on Enterprise with Finance approval |
| Termination for convenience | Either party on 90 days' written notice; prepaid fees for the remaining term are not refunded except where Kestrel terminates | **[Legal approval required]** to shorten notice or add refunds |
| Limitation of liability | Each party's aggregate liability capped at **fees paid or payable in the 12 months** preceding the claim; excludes indirect and consequential loss | **[Legal approval required]** for any increase |
| Unlimited liability | **Not offered by default**, including for data breaches; carve-outs from the cap are limited to those required by law (fraud, death or personal injury, wilful misconduct) | **[Legal approval required]**; requests for uncapped data-breach liability are escalated to the General Counsel and CEO |
| Indemnification by Kestrel | IP infringement indemnity: Kestrel defends and indemnifies claims that the platform infringes third-party IP, with standard exclusions (customer modifications, combinations, misuse) | Other indemnities (data breach, regulatory fines, general third-party claims) are **negotiated case by case [Legal approval required]** |
| Governing law and venue | Laws of the Netherlands; courts of Amsterdam | English law available for UK customers; other jurisdictions **[Legal approval required]** |
| Service levels | 99.9% monthly availability with 5%/10%/25% service credits as sole remedy | 99.95% target on Enterprise **[Legal approval required]** |
| Data protection | DPA with SCCs; 48-hour breach notification; 30-day deletion | Deviations **[Legal approval required]** |
| Audit rights | Once per 12 months on 30 days' notice, or reliance on SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / pen test summary; customer bears its own costs | More frequent audits **[Legal approval required]** |

## 3. Financial standing

Kestrel Cloud B.V. (KvK 74812993) has been profitable since 2025, closed a €28 million Series B in 2024, and carries no debt. Audited annual accounts are filed with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce; a summary financial statement and a letter from the auditors confirming going-concern status can be shared under NDA.

## 4. Regulatory and litigation status

Kestrel is not subject to any pending litigation, regulatory investigation, or enforcement action, and has never received a supervisory authority fine.

## 5. Guidance for questionnaire responses

When a questionnaire asks about liability caps, unlimited liability, indemnities, pricing, or termination, respond with the standard position above and note that variations are subject to negotiation and Legal approval. Do not commit to uncapped liability, broader indemnities, or non-standard payment terms in a questionnaire response.
