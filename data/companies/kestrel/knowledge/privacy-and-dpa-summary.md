# Privacy Programme and Data Processing Agreement Summary

**Owner:** Elena Rossi, Data Protection Officer (dpo@kestrelcloud.example) | **DPA version:** 4.2 (March 2026)

## 1. Roles and legal basis

For the Kestrel Platform, the customer is the **controller** of the data it connects, and Kestrel Cloud B.V. is the **processor**, acting only on the customer's documented instructions as set out in the Data Processing Agreement (DPA). For account, billing, and support data about customer personnel, Kestrel is an independent controller under its own Privacy Notice. Kestrel is established in the Netherlands and is subject to the GDPR and Dutch implementing law; the lead supervisory authority is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. For UK customers, the DPA incorporates the UK GDPR and the ICO International Data Transfer Addendum.

## 2. Data Processing Agreement

The DPA is a standard, pre-signed document available from the Trust Portal and forms part of every subscription agreement. Key terms:

- **Instructions and purpose limitation:** processing only to provide the services; no processing for Kestrel's own purposes beyond aggregated, non-identifiable service metrics.
- **Confidentiality:** all personnel bound by confidentiality obligations and trained annually.
- **Security:** the technical and organisational measures in Annex II, aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the SOC 2 Type II controls.
- **Subprocessors:** general authorisation with a published list, 30 days' prior notice of additions, and a right to object with termination and pro-rata refund as the remedy.
- **International transfers:** EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914, Module 2 controller-to-processor and Module 3 processor-to-processor) are incorporated; transfer impact assessments are maintained for each non-EU subprocessor.
- **Breach notification:** Kestrel notifies the customer of a confirmed personal data breach affecting its data **within 48 hours** of confirmation, with sufficient detail to support the customer's 72-hour notification to its authority.
- **Assistance:** Kestrel supports data subject requests, DPIAs, and prior consultation, within 10 business days for standard requests.
- **Deletion and return:** customer data is deleted within 30 days of termination, with backups purged within a further 35 days; self-service export is available throughout the term and for 30 days after.
- **Audit:** customers may audit Kestrel once per 12 months with 30 days' written notice, or rely on the SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certificate, and penetration test summary. Audits may be performed by the customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality.

Negotiated deviations from the standard DPA require Legal review.

## 3. Data residency

Customer data is stored and processed in the EU by default (eu-central-1 with DR in eu-west-1). US customers may elect us-east-1 hosting, available since Q1 2026; in that case all customer data, backups, and logs for the tenant remain in the US. Kestrel does not move tenant data between regions without written customer instruction.

## 4. Data subject rights

Kestrel supports customers in responding to data subject access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection requests. Tenant administrators can search for and delete an individual's records across integrated datasets using the Subject Locator tool, which produces an auditable report. Requests received directly by Kestrel from data subjects of a customer are forwarded to that customer within 3 business days.

## 5. Commitments on data use

- Kestrel does not sell customer data or personal data to anyone.
- Customer data is never used to train AI or machine learning models, including for Kestrel Assist.
- Customer data is not used for advertising or profiling.
- Kestrel personnel access customer data only for support, incident response, or as instructed, with every access logged and reportable to the customer.

## 6. Governance

- Records of processing activities (ROPA) are maintained under Article 30 for both controller and processor activities and reviewed semi-annually.
- Data protection impact assessments are performed for new high-risk processing; a DPIA for Kestrel Assist was completed in January 2026.
- Privacy by design is embedded in the SDLC through the DPO's review of Design Docs that introduce new personal data processing.
- Annual privacy training is mandatory for all staff.
- Kestrel has not received any regulatory enquiry, complaint, or fine from a supervisory authority.
