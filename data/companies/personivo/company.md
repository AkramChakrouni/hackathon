# Personivo B.V.

Personivo B.V. is a 25-person HR SaaS company in Utrecht, the Netherlands, founded to serve regulated employers. The Personivo Platform handles employee onboarding, employee records and contract management for banks and insurers across Europe. The platform is hosted on Microsoft Azure in West Europe (Netherlands) with backups replicated to Azure North Europe (Ireland).

The CTO acts as Information Security Officer. Personivo runs an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified ISMS (since May 2024) with annual external surveillance audits and an annual internal audit. Security, data protection and incident response / business continuity are governed by three policies (current versions effective 1 June 2026). Past questionnaire answers from 2025 exist but may be outdated; current policies prevail when they differ.

Customers are European financial institutions; questionnaires are typically CAIQ v4.1 (CSA STAR) or a bank's own vendor assessment.
