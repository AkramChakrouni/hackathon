# Personivo B.V. Data Protection Policy

Document owner: Data Protection Officer
Version: 3.1
Effective: 1 March 2026
Next review: March 2027
Classification: Internal

## 1. Roles

For personal data that customers process in the Personivo Platform, Personivo acts as processor under the GDPR and processes data only on the documented instructions of the customer, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement (DPA). For personal data of its own employees and business contacts, Personivo is controller. Personivo has appointed an external Data Protection Officer (dpo@personivo.example).

## 2. Data processed

The platform processes personal data of customer employees and job candidates, including contact details, employment contracts, salary information, national identification numbers where required for payroll, and bank account numbers. All customer data is classified as Strictly Confidential.

## 3. Data location and transfers

All customer data is stored and processed in the European Economic Area: primary storage in Azure West Europe (Netherlands) and backups in Azure North Europe (Ireland). Personivo does not transfer customer data outside the EEA. Personivo staff access customer data only from within the EU, only when needed for support, and only with the customer's permission, which is logged per request.

## 4. Sub-processors

Personivo uses the following sub-processors, each bound by a DPA with equivalent obligations:

* Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd: cloud hosting (EU regions only)
* Mailjet SAS (France): transactional email
* Sentry, EU data region (Germany): application error monitoring, with personal data scrubbed before sending

Customers are notified at least 30 days before a new sub-processor is added and may object. The sub-processor list is reviewed annually.

## 5. Inventory and data flows

Personivo maintains a record of processing activities (Article 30 GDPR) as its inventory of personal and sensitive data, and data flow diagrams showing where data is collected, stored and transmitted. Both are reviewed at least annually and upon significant changes.

## 6. Privacy by design and DPIAs

New features and new processing are assessed for privacy impact during design. A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is performed for any processing likely to result in a high risk. The DPIA for the core platform was completed in 2024 and last reviewed in November 2025. Privacy friendly settings are enabled by default.

## 7. Data subject rights

Requests from data subjects about customer data are forwarded to the relevant customer within two business days. The platform gives customer administrators tools to view, export, correct and delete personal data, so the customer can fulfil requests under Articles 15 to 17 GDPR.

## 8. Non-production use

Production data is never copied to development, test or staging environments. These environments use synthetic data only.

## 9. Retention and deletion

Customer data is deleted within 30 days after termination of the contract. Deleted data is removed from backups when those backups expire, at most 35 days later. Deletion in Azure uses cryptographic erasure, and laptops are wiped before reuse or disposal. Personivo's own HR records are retained according to statutory retention periods.

## 10. Personal data breaches

Personivo notifies affected customers of a personal data breach without undue delay and at the latest 24 hours after confirming it, so customers can meet their 72 hour notification duty towards the supervisory authority. Details are set out in the Incident Response and Business Continuity Policy.
